# Design Files for Air Defender

This document lists the public Tinkercad designs used for the physical components of Air Defender (enclosures, holders, signage, and controller panels).

- Wall-mount LED profile holder:
  - https://www.tinkercad.com/things/h7WEBlRsnWd-wall-mount-air-defender-led-profile-holder
  - Purpose: Aluminum-profile-mounted LED strip holder for the vertical wall installation.

- Controller panel:
  - https://www.tinkercad.com/things/kae1EvzGFgT-air-defender-controller-panel
  - Purpose: Hexagonal controller enclosure and panel layout for the three arcade buttons and LCD.

- Industrial tower toppers / controller arrows:
  - https://www.tinkercad.com/things/0YIkiUAXub7-industrial-tower-toppers-controller-arrows
  - Purpose: Decorative toppers and arrow indicators used on the wall-mounted LED towers.

- Air Defender sign / branding:
  - https://www.tinkercad.com/things/e2Fd5e4X3Hg-air-defender-sign
  - Purpose: Branded sign used above the wall display.

Notes:
- These are public Tinkercad projects; you can duplicate or export STL files directly from Tinkercad for local 3D printing.
- If you'd like, I can download the STL exports and add them to `assets/designs/` in the repo (recommended for archiving and offline access).
